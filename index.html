PLATE III · THE EQUIPMENT

Your face tells me your secrets.

In 1872, Darwin noticed that a child born blind, who has never once seen a smile, smiles anyway, and smiles the same way. A century later Ekman carried photographs into the highlands of New Guinea and found strangers read grief and anger as easily as their neighbors did.

Expression is not culture. It is equipment.

The human face has 43 distinct muscles. From the moment you are born, they signal your every response to the world. Within the persistent pattern of your early years, your whole emotional experience gets traced across your face, day after day.

The good, the bad, and the ugly all become indelible markers of who you are. That is why I can look at you at 35 and reconstruct the first five years of your life.

“your face has been tracking your emotional life since before you were born.”

PLATE IV · A SCIENTIFIC TRAIL YOU CAN RELY ON

The man who discovered this never got to publish it.

Dr. Michael J. Lincoln was a clinical psychologist. Handed a ward of emotionally disturbed children nobody could control, he sat in the chaos and wrote down, second by second, everything they did. Then he began seeing a child’s whole file in the child’s face, before intake, before a word.

He spent a decade mapping which regions of the face carry which chapters of the story, checking every reading against the clinical assessments. They held. But the work was too strange for the journals of his day, so the map lives as a hypothesis: tested across decades of practice, never through peer review.

PLATE V · THE BOUNDARIES

The rules empower you.

The boundaries are half the practice. Dr. Lincoln built this work so we could see each other comprehensively and compassionately. Everybody deserves to be seen.

BOUNDARY 01

A reading is not therapy.

I am not a therapist. A reading is one part of a balanced mix of support. If something heavy surfaces, I name it plainly and point you to the right professional.

BOUNDARY 02

Not a lie detector, not fortune telling.

It is not a way to get something over on somebody. This work is for understanding a person, never for cornering one.

BOUNDARY 03

A reading is not a sentencing.

Nothing in your face limits you. An archetype names what is most alive in you right now, and every archetype is open to every person.
